About the Celebration

Ganpati in
the Mountains

Ganesh Utsav is IIT Mandi's biggest flagship festival — a celebration that brings students, teachers, and the wider IIT Mandi community together in devotion and joy. Surrounded by the majestic Himalayas, our celebration of Lord Ganesha carries a unique character found nowhere else.

Here, ancient devotion meets mountain serenity. The golden figure of Ganpati rises against pine and snow — a sight that turns the festival into a journey of the soul.

  • IIT Mandi's biggest flagship festival
  • Students, faculty & community united in celebration
  • A uniquely Himalayan Ganpati experience
The Experience

A Celebration Like No Other

Each moment of the utsav is crafted to honour tradition within the embrace of the mountains.

🪔

Devotion

Aarti, bhajans, and prayers that resonate across the valley as the evening mist settles over the hills.

🎭

Culture

Music, dance, and performances that celebrate the rich heritage of Indian festivals in the Himalayan spirit.

🤝

Community

Students and teachers together — building bonds, sharing prasad, and creating memories against the mountain backdrop.

🏔️

The Mountains

Ganpati worshipped in the shadow of the Himalayas — a divine presence framed by snow-capped peaks.

🎶

Sound & Song

The gentle rhythm of dhol and the sweetness of devotional songs carried on the cool mountain air.

💫

Togetherness

A festival that belongs to every member of the IIT Mandi family — a celebration of unity and gratitude.
